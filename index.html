Dice Duel - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Android game Dice Duel (package com.njake.diceduel, "the game") handles information. The game is made by an independent developer ("I", "me").

1. Information I collect

I do not collect, store or receive any personal information from you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. I do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress, settings (volume, graphics, vibration), achievements, unlocked colours and statistics are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app or use "Delete all data" in the game settings, it is removed.

3. Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads using Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch an ad, and watching one gives you a bonus reward in the game. AdMob, a service of Google, may collect and use certain information to show and measure ads, for example your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This data is handled by Google and not by me. You can learn more in Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Google Play services

The game uses Google Play services, for example to tell you when a new version of the game is available. This is handled by Google under its own Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing of information

I do not sell, rent or share personal information, because I do not collect any. The only third party involved is Google (advertising and Google Play), as described above.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Security

Because the game keeps your data only on your device, I do not hold any of your data on a server. Keep your device secure to keep your game data secure.

8. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date shown above.
